FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

THE IMPORTANT STUFF, BEFORE YOU BOOK.

If you are unsure whether InfoChain is right for you, start here. These are the questions we expect people to have before their first session.

01 — THE BASICS

START HERE.

Crypto can feel risky simply because you do not know what you are looking at. The answers below explain exactly what InfoChain does — and what it deliberately does not do.

01

Do you hold client funds?

No. Never. InfoChain is an education and consulting service. We do not take custody of crypto, private keys or seed phrases. You keep control of your accounts and sign your own transactions.

02

Is this financial or investment advice?

No. We explain how crypto tools, protocols, transactions and risks work. We do not manage portfolios, tell you what to buy or sell, or promise returns. Any discussion of portfolio structure is educational only.

03

Who is InfoChain for?

Beginners who are about to enter crypto, existing users who still feel unsure about wallets and networks, and curious people who want to understand DeFi and on-chain tools before using them.

04

Can sessions be in Danish?

Yes. Sessions can be held in Danish or English. The language toggle on the website is also available so you can read the core information in either language.

05

What should I bring to a session?

Bring the question that is confusing you. If relevant, have the wallet, exchange or protocol interface you want to understand open on your own device. Never send us passwords, seed phrases or private keys.

06

Can you help with a transaction or wallet setup?

Yes, educationally. We can explain what each step means, what to check and what common mistakes look like. You remain the person operating the account and approving the transaction.

07

How long are sessions?

Package 01 is 90 minutes, Package 02 is 120 minutes, and Package 03 provides lifetime private support. If you are unsure where to start, contact us and describe what you want to learn.

08

What if I know almost nothing about crypto?

That is exactly what Package 01 is for. There is no test and no expected starting level. We can begin with the basics and move at a pace that makes sense to you.

STILL UNSURE?
