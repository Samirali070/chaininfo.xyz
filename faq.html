FAQ

Common questions before booking.

Short answers that reduce confusion and explain how ChainInfo works.

Do you hold client funds?

No. ChainInfo is education only. We never ask for seed phrases, private keys or custody of funds.

Is this personal financial advice?

No. ChainInfo explains tools, risks and concepts. The goal is education and safer understanding, not personalised investment advice.

Who is it for?

Beginners, curious learners and people who want to understand wallets, exchanges and DeFi before using them.

Can sessions be in Danish?

Yes. Sessions can be held in either Danish or English.

Educational content only. No financial advice, no custody, no guaranteed returns.
