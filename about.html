ABOUT INFOCHAIN

CRYPTO EDUCATION THAT FEELS HUMAN, PRACTICAL AND CLEAR.

InfoChain is built for people who want to understand crypto before they start clicking around. We turn confusing wallets, exchanges, DeFi tools and on-chain processes into practical steps you can actually follow.

THE MISSION

MAKE CRYPTO LESS CONFUSING.

Crypto often feels difficult because beginners are expected to learn ten things at once. InfoChain takes a different approach: start with the exact thing you are trying to do, understand why it works, then build from there.

01 — SIMPLE

Explain it like a human.

No unnecessary jargon. We break down addresses, networks, approvals, bridges and protocols into language you can use.

02 — PRACTICAL

Learn by doing.

Sessions are built around real questions: setting up a wallet, checking a transaction, understanding a network or navigating a DeFi interface.

03 — CONTROL

You stay in control.

We never need your seed phrase, private keys or custody of your funds. You click, sign and control your own accounts.

HOW IT WORKS

NO CLASSROOM. NO PERFORMANCE. JUST YOUR QUESTIONS.

Before a session, you tell us what you are trying to understand. During the session, we work through the relevant tools and concepts at your pace. Afterwards, you leave with a clearer mental model and practical next steps instead of a pile of theory.
